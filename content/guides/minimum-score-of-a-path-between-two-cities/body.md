## Component minimum

1. Build adjacency lists; BFS or DFS from city 1 to mark its component.
2. Return the smallest `distance` over roads whose endpoints are marked.

Examples → 5, 2.

A path may repeat roads and cities, so walking out to any road in the component and back costs nothing extra in score. Cities 1 and n are guaranteed connected, so this component contains n. Union-find over all roads works too.

Time: O(n + m)  
Space: O(n + m)
