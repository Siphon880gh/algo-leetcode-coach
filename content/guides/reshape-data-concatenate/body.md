## concat

1. Return `pd.concat([df1, df2])`; add `ignore_index=True` for a fresh 0 … n − 1 index.

The example → students 1 … 6 in order. Checked in pandas 2.2.

axis=1 places the frames side by side instead (4 rows × 6 columns here).

Time: O(n + m)  
Space: O(n + m)
