## Consecutive after sorting

Sort; return `min(a[i + k − 1] − a[i])` for `i = 0 … n − k`.

`[90], 1` → 0. `[9,4,1,7], 2` → 2.

Any chosen set's spread is at least that of the consecutive block between its min and max. `k = 1` gives 0.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(1) besides sorting
