## Round half up

1. Return `100 − ((p + 5) // 10) · 10`.

Examples → 90, 80, 90.

Language rounding may go to even: Python’s round(2.5) is 2, so p = 25 → 80 instead of 70.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
