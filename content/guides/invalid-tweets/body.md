## Length filter

1. `SELECT tweet_id FROM Tweets WHERE CHAR_LENGTH(content) > 15`.

Example: tweet 1 has 11 characters (valid); tweet 2 is longer than 15 → `2`.

`LENGTH` counts bytes in MySQL and can differ for multi-byte characters; `CHAR_LENGTH` counts characters. The threshold is strict (> 15).

Time: O(rows · len)  
Space: O(rows)
