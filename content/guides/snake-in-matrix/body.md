## Index steps

1. p = 0. UP: p −= n; DOWN: p += n; LEFT: p −= 1; RIGHT: p += 1.

Examples → 3, 1.

Treating UP as + n: example 2 → 7 instead of 1.

Time: O(len(commands))  
Space: O(1)
