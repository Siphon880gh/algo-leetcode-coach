## Two lower bounds

Return `max(max(amount), ceil(sum(amount) / 2))`.

Examples → 4, 7, 5. Checked against a full search over every amount up to 6.

If one type has more cups than the other two together, each second fills at most one of it. Otherwise repeatedly filling the two largest piles keeps them balanced, so only the final second can fill a single cup.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
