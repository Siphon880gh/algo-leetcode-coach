## Group by count

1. cnt = letter counts; groups[k] = letters with count k.
2. Pick k maximizing (len(groups[k]), k).
3. Return the letters of groups[k].

Examples → "ab", "abcd", "fp". Checked against a direct search on 2,000 random strings.

Breaking ties toward the smaller frequency: example 3 → "gi" instead of "fp".

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
