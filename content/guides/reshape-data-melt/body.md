## melt

1. Return `pd.melt(report, id_vars=['product'], var_name='quarter', value_name='sales')`.

The example → 8 rows, quarter by quarter: Umbrella quarter_1 417, SleepingBag quarter_1 800, …. Checked in pandas 2.2.

Without var_name and value_name the columns are called variable and value.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
