## Largest first

1. t = Σ apple. Sort capacity descending; take boxes until their sum ≥ t; return the count.

Examples → 2, 4.

Smallest first is wrong: example 1 → 3 instead of 2.

Time: O(m log m)  
Space: O(1) besides sorting
