## Closed form

1. sumOdd = n², sumEven = n(n + 1).
2. Consecutive numbers are coprime, so the GCD is n.

Examples → 4, 5. Checked against the direct sums for every n up to 1,000.

Summing odd and even numbers up to n (not the first n of each): n = 4 → 2 instead of 4.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
