## Prefix odd counts

1. Return `(high + 1) // 2 − low // 2`.

low 3, high 7 → 3 (3, 5, 7). low 8, high 10 → 1 (9).

Do not loop; high can be 10^9. Do not use `(high − low) / 2` alone; it is off by one when both ends are odd.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
