## Top two

1. Sort the digits of n.
2. Answer the last two multiplied.

Examples → 3, 4, 8. Checked against every pair of positions on 300 random numbers.

Squaring the largest digit: example 1 → 9 instead of 3.

Time: O(d log d), d ≤ 10 digits  
Space: O(d)
