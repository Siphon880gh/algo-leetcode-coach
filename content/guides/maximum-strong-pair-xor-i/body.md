## Brute force

1. For every x, y in nums: if `|x − y| ≤ min(x, y)`, update the answer with `x XOR y`.

Examples → 7, 0, 7.

A pair of a number with itself is always strong and gives 0, so the answer is never negative. For n up to 5 · 10^4 see part II.

Time: O(n²)  
Space: O(1)
