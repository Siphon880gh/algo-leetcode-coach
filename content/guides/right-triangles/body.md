## Corner counting

1. r[i] = ones in row i; c[j] = ones in column j.
2. Answer = Σ over ones of (r[i] − 1)(c[j] − 1).

Examples → 2, 0, 2.

The total can exceed 10^11; use 64-bit.

Time: O(m n)  
Space: O(m + n)
