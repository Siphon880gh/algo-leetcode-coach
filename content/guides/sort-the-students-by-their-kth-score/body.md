## Sort rows

1. Sort `score` by `row[k]`, largest first.
2. Return it.

Examples → [[7,5,11,2],[10,6,9,1],[4,8,3,15]], [[5,6],[3,4]].

Sorting the whole rows keeps each student’s scores together; sorting column k alone would mix students.

Time: O(m log m + m · n)  
Space: O(m)
