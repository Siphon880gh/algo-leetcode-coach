## Only parity matters

1. `odd` = chips at odd positions, `even` = chips at even positions.
2. Return `min(odd, even)`.

`[1,2,3]` → 1. `[2,2,2,3,3]` → 2. `[1,1000000000]` → 1.

Do not simulate moves or pick a target position; distance is irrelevant. Do not count distinct positions; count chips.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
