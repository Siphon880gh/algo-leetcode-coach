## Direct simulation

1. Repeat k times: i = first index of the minimum; nums[i] *= multiplier.

Examples → [8, 4, 6, 5, 6], [16, 8].

Taking the last minimum on ties: example 1 → [4, 8, 6, 5, 6] instead of [8, 4, 6, 5, 6].

Time: O(k n)  
Space: O(1)
