## Two flags

1. `bulky = max(length, width, height) ≥ 10^4` or `length · width · height ≥ 10^9`.
2. `heavy = mass ≥ 100`.
3. Return Both, Bulky, Heavy or Neither from the flags.

Examples → Heavy, Neither.

The volume can reach 10^15, so multiply in 64-bit. Both comparisons are ≥, not >.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
