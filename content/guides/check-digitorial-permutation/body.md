## One sum

1. f = Σ d! over the digits of n.
2. Return sorted(digits of f) = sorted(digits of n).

Examples → true, false. Checked against trying every permutation for n < 3,000, 300 random n and the known factorions.

Checking only n itself: constructed check (run in code) n = 154 → false instead of true.

Time: O(log n)  
Space: O(log n)
