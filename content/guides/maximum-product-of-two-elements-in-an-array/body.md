## Top two

1. Track `m1 ≥ m2` while scanning.
2. Return `(m1 − 1) · (m2 − 1)`.

`[3,4,5,2]` → 12. `[1,5,4,5]` → 16 (duplicates count). `[3,7]` → 12.

Do not require distinct values; the two indices must differ, not the values.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
