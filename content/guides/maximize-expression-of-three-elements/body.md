## Sort

1. Sort nums.
2. Return nums[n − 1] + nums[n − 2] − nums[0].

Examples → 8, 11. Checked against trying every triple on 3,000 random arrays.

Using the maximum twice: example 1 → 9 instead of 8.

Time: O(n log n), or O(n) with one pass  
Space: O(1)
