## Positions by value

1. pos[v] = indices of v in order.
2. answer = min over v and t of 2 · (pos[v][t + 2] − pos[v][t]).
3. Return it, or −1.

Examples → 6, 8, −1. Checked against testing every triple on 3,000 random arrays.

Forgetting the factor 2: example 1 → 3 instead of 6.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
