## Formula

1. a, b = the two largest |nums[i]|.
2. Return 10^5 · a · b.

Examples → 3500000, 1200000, 0. Checked against trying every replacement value on 400 random cases (with a smaller bound in place of 10^5).

Always replacing with +10^5: example 1 → 0 instead of 3500000.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
