## Flood fill

1. For each unvisited positive cell, DFS or BFS over 4-neighbours with positive values, summing them.
2. Count islands whose sum % k = 0.

Examples → 2, 6.

Joining cells diagonally too: example 2 → 1 instead of 6.

Time: O(m · n)  
Space: O(m · n)
