## One pass

1. mx = max(lights).
2. answer = max(period − r) over cars with r = arrivalTime mod period ≥ mx, or 0.

Examples → 5, 1, 3. Matched the best light for each car on 1,000 random cases.

Treating r = mx as green: example 3 → 2 instead of 3.

Time: O(n + m)  
Space: O(1)
