## Split and convert

1. Return the join of bin(int(part)) without the prefix for year, month and day.

Examples → 100000100000-10-11101, 11101101100-1-1.

Python’s bin keeps its 0b prefix: example 2 → 0b11101101100-0b1-0b1.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
