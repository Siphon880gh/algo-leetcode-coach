## Group the most expensive together

Sort descending; `ans = Σ cost[i]` for `i mod 3 ≠ 2`.

Examples → 5, 23, 10.

The free candy can cost at most the cheaper of the two bought, so grouping the two priciest with the next one frees the largest candy possible each time.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(1) besides sorting
