## Leftmost 6

1. Convert to a string, replace the first `6` with `9`, convert back.

`9669` → `9969`. `9996` → `9999`. `9999` → `9999`.

Do not replace every 6; only one change is allowed. Do not change a 9 into a 6; that only lowers the number.

Time: O(digits)  
Space: O(digits)
