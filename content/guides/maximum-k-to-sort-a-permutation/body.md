## One AND

1. If nums[i] = i for all i, return 0.
2. Return the AND of nums[i] over every i with nums[i] ≠ i.

Examples → 1, 2, 0. Checked against searching every reachable order for every k on all sizes up to 6.

Taking the AND of every value: example 1 → 0 instead of 1.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
