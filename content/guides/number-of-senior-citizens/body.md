## Fixed positions

1. For each detail string: `age = int(s[11..12])`.
2. Count ages strictly greater than 60.

Examples → 2, 0.

The layout is fixed: 10 phone digits, a gender character at index 10, the age at 11–12 and the seat at 13–14. Strictly greater means 60 itself does not count.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
