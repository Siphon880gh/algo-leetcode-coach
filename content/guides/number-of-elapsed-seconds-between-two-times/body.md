## Convert

1. sec(t) = HH · 3600 + MM · 60 + SS.
2. Return sec(endTime) − sec(startTime).

Examples → 25, 1504.

Subtracting the digits as plain numbers: example 2 → 6544 instead of 1504.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
