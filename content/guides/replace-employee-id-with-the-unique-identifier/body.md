## Keep everyone

```sql
SELECT u.unique_id, e.name
FROM Employees e
LEFT JOIN EmployeeUNI u ON u.id = e.id;
```

In the example, Alice and Bob have no unique id → `null`; Meir → 2, Winston → 3, Jonathan → 1.

Do not inner-join; employees without a unique id would vanish. Do not start from EmployeeUNI; the employee list drives the output.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
