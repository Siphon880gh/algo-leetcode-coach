## Min and max bound the dates

```sql
SELECT p.product_id, p.product_name
FROM Product p
JOIN Sales s ON p.product_id = s.product_id
GROUP BY p.product_id, p.product_name
HAVING MIN(s.sale_date) >= '2019-01-01'
   AND MAX(s.sale_date) <= '2019-03-31';
```

The inner join drops products with no sales, which should not be reported.

Do not filter with `WHERE sale_date BETWEEN …` before grouping; a product that also sold later would still appear. The range is inclusive on both ends.

Time: O(n) with hashing  
Space: O(products)
