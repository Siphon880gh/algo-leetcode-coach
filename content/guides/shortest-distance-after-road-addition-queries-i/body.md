## Repeat BFS

1. Start with roads i → i + 1.
2. Per query add u → v and BFS from 0; record dist[n − 1].

Examples → [3, 2, 1], [1, 1].

Subtracting v − u − 1 from the last answer: example 1 → [3, 2, −1].

Time: O(q (n + q))  
Space: O(n + q)
