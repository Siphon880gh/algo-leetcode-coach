## Per value maximum

1. best[x] = max y over indices with that x.
2. If fewer than 3 keys, return −1; else the sum of the three largest values in best.

Examples → 14, −1. Checked against every triplet on 300 random arrays.

Taking the three largest y overall: example 1 → 15 instead of 14.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(n)
