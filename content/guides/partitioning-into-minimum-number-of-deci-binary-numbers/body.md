## Largest digit

1. Return the largest digit in `n`.

`"32"` → 3. `"82734"` → 8. `"27346209830709182346"` → 9.

Construction: the k-th number has a 1 wherever the digit is ≥ k. Do not convert n to an integer; it can have 10^5 digits.

Time: O(len)  
Space: O(1)
