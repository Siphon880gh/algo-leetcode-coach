## Enumerate

1. For every i ≤ j: test whether `nums[:i] + nums[j + 1:]` is strictly increasing.

Examples → 10, 7, 3.

Strictly: allowing equal neighbours gives example 3 → 4 instead of 3. Part II solves larger inputs with two pointers.

Time: O(n³)  
Space: O(n)
