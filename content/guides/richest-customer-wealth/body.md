## Row sums

1. Return `max(sum(row) for row in accounts)`.

`[[1,2,3],[3,2,1]]` → 6. `[[1,5],[7,3],[3,5]]` → 10. `[[2,8,7],[7,1,3],[1,9,5]]` → 17.

Values are small (≤ 50 · 100), so no overflow concerns.

Time: O(m · n)  
Space: O(1)
