## Scan

1. r = int(reversed digits of n); lo, hi = min, max.
2. Return Σ x for x in [lo, hi] that are prime.

Examples → 132, 17, 0.

Excluding both ends: example 1 → 88 instead of 132.

Time: O(R √R), R ≤ 1000  
Space: O(1)
