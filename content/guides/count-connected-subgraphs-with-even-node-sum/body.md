## Enumerate

1. adj[i] = bitmask of neighbors.
2. For each non-empty mask with an even sum: flood from its lowest bit inside the mask; count it if the flood covers the mask.

Examples → 2, 0.

Skipping the connectivity check: example 1 → 3 instead of 2.

Time: O(2^n · n)  
Space: O(n)
