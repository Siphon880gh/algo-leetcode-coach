## Count valid starts

1. Add s[r]; while count[s[r]] ≥ k, drop s[l] and l += 1.
2. ans += l.

Examples → 4, 15. Checked against every substring on 300 random cases.

Counting a character that appears exactly k times: aaa, k = 2 → 2 instead of 3.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(26)
