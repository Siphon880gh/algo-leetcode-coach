## Direct check

1. For each i: good if (i − k < 0 or nums[i] > nums[i − k]) and (i + k ≥ n or nums[i] > nums[i + k]).
2. Answer the sum of good elements.

Examples → 12, 2.

Using ≥ instead of >: [2, 2], k = 1 → 4 instead of 0.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
