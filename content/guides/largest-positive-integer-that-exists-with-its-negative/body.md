## Check −k

1. `seen = set(nums)`.
2. Return the largest `k > 0` in `seen` with `−k` in `seen`, or −1.

Examples → 3, 7, −1.

Sorting and moving two pointers in from both ends also works. Values lie in −1000 … 1000, so a boolean array of size 2001 can replace the set.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
