## Build

1. c = count of 1s.
2. Return `1 × (c − 1) + 0 × zeros + 1`.

Examples → 001, 1001. Checked against trying every arrangement of small strings.

Sorting descending gives the largest number but it may end in 0.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
