## Three lookups per value

`c = Counter(nums)`; return `[x for x in c if c[x] == 1 and x − 1 not in c and x + 1 not in c]`.

Examples → `[10,8]`, `[1,5]` (any order).

Example 2 rules out 3 because it appears twice, even though 2 and 4 are absent.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
