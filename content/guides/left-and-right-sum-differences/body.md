## Total minus left

1. `total = Σ nums`; `left = 0`.
2. For each `i`: `right = total − left − nums[i]`; output `|left − right|`; `left += nums[i]`.

Examples → [15,1,11,22], [0].

Sums are at most 10^8, so 32-bit is enough.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1) besides the output
