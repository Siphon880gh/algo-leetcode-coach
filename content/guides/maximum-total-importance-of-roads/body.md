## Rearrangement

1. Degree of every city.
2. Sort ascending; return `Σ (i + 1) · deg[i]`.

Examples → 43, 20.

Pairing larger values with larger degrees maximises the sum by the rearrangement inequality. With n = 5 · 10^4 and many roads the total exceeds 32-bit.

Time: O(n log n + E)  
Space: O(n)
