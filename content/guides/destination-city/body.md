## Sources set

1. `src = {a for a, b in paths}`.
2. Return the `b` not in `src`.

`[["London","New York"],["New York","Lima"],["Lima","Sao Paulo"]]` → `"Sao Paulo"`. `[["B","C"],["D","B"],["C","A"]]` → `"A"`.

Do not assume paths come in travel order. Do not count in-degree only; the answer must have out-degree 0.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
