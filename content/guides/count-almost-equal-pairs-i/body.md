## Try every swap

1. For each pair, pad to the longer length.
2. Count it if y appears among x and its single-swap variants, or the reverse.

Examples → 2, 10, 0.

Comparing only equal-length numbers drops 3 and 30: example 1 → 1 instead of 2.

Time: O(n² · d²) with d ≤ 7 digits  
Space: O(d²)
