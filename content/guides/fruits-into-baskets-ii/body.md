## Scan

1. For each fruit: find the first unused basket with capacity ≥ the quantity; mark it used, or count the fruit as unplaced.

Examples → 1, 0. Checked against a segment-tree version on 1,000 random arrays.

Choosing the smallest basket that fits: fruits = [2, 9, 3, 5, 5], baskets = [8, 4, 3, 4, 8] → 1 instead of 2.

Time: O(n²)  
Space: O(n)
