## Try the next few

1. For x = n, n + 1, …: if the product of x’s digits mod t = 0, return x.

Examples → 10, 16. Every n ≤ 100 and t ≤ 10 finishes within 10 numbers (checked in code).

Skipping numbers that contain a 0: example 1 → 12 instead of 10.

Time: O(10 · log n)  
Space: O(1)
