## Promise plus timer

1. `return new Promise(resolve => setTimeout(resolve, millis))`.

Examples → resolves after about 100 ms and 200 ms. Checked in Node by timing an await.

The function can also be declared async; the caller awaits the returned promise either way. Timers can fire a little late, which the statement allows.

Time: O(1) work, millis of waiting  
Space: O(1)
