## Convert

1. to_base(x, b): repeat x % b → digit, x //= b; reverse.
2. Answer to_base(n², 16) + to_base(n³, 36).

Examples → A91P1, 5101000.

Swapping the bases: example 1 → 4P895 instead of A91P1.

Time: O(log n)  
Space: O(log n)
