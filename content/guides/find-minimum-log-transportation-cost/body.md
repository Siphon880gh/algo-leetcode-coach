## One cut

1. For each log x > k: add k · (x − k).
2. Answer the sum (0 if neither log is too long).

Examples → 5, 0. Checked against trying every cut on 500 random cases.

Cutting the long log in half: example 1 → 9 instead of 5.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
