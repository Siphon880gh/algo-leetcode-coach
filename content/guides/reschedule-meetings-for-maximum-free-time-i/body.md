## Window over gaps

1. gaps = [startTime[0], startTime[i] − endTime[i − 1] …, eventTime − endTime[n − 1]].
2. Answer the largest sum of k + 1 consecutive gaps.

Examples → 2, 6, 0. Checked against trying every integer schedule on 200 random small cases.

A window of only k gaps: example 2 → 5 instead of 6.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
