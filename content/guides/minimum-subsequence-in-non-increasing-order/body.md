## Largest first

1. Sort descending; `total = Σ`.
2. Append elements, adding to `taken`, until `taken > total − taken`.

`[4,3,10,9,8]` → `[10,9]`. `[4,4,7,6,7]` → `[7,7,6]`.

Do not stop when the sums are equal; the condition is strictly greater. Do not return the elements in input order; the answer is non-increasing.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(n)
