## Two pointers

1. Grow r and count zeros and ones.
2. While both counts exceed k, move l.
3. Add r − l + 1.

Examples → 12, 25, 15. Checked against every substring on 300 random strings.

Requiring both counts ≤ k: example 1 → 9 instead of 12.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
