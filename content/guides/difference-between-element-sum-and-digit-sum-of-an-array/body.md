## Two sums

1. `e = Σ nums`.
2. `d = Σ` of the digits of each element, by repeated `mod 10` and `/ 10`.
3. Return `|e − d|`.

Examples → 9, 0.

A number is at least the sum of its digits, so e − d ≥ 0. Totals stay below 4 · 10^6.

Time: O(n · digits)  
Space: O(1)
