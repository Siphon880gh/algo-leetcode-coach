## Sort the set

1. Return sorted(set(nums), descending)[:k].

Examples → [100, 93, 90], [100, 93, 84], [2, 1].

Not removing duplicates: example 2 → [100, 93, 93].

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(n)
