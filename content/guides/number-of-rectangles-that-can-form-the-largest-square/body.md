## One pass

1. `s = min(l, w)` per rectangle.
2. Track `best` and how many times it occurs; reset the count on a new maximum.

`[[5,8],[3,9],[5,12],[16,5]]` → 3 (side 5). `[[2,3],[3,7],[4,3],[3,7]]` → 3 (side 3).

Do not use the max side; a square must fit inside the rectangle.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
