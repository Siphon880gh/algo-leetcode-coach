## Sorted neighbours

1. Sort nums.
2. Return `min(nums[i + 1] − nums[i])`.

Examples → 1, 9. Checked against every split on 300 random small arrays.

For any partition, max(nums1) and min(nums2) are two different elements, and two different values differ by at least the smallest adjacent gap in sorted order; splitting there achieves it.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(1)
