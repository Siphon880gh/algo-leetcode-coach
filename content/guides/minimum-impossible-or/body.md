## Powers of two

1. Put `nums` in a set; `p = 1`.
2. While `p` is in the set, `p = 2p`.
3. Return `p`.

Examples → 4, 1. Checked against every subsequence OR on 300 random arrays.

Every number below p is an OR of the powers below p, which are all present. Values up to 10^9 mean p is at most 2^30.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
