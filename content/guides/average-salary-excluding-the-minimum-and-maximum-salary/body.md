## Sum minus extremes

1. Track `total`, `lo`, and `hi` in one pass.
2. Return `(total − lo − hi) / (n − 2)` as a float.

`[4000,3000,1000,2000]` → 2500.0. `[1000,2000,3000]` → 2000.0.

Do not use integer division. Sorting also works, but is unnecessary.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
