## Two heaviest each round

1. Push every stone into a max heap (negate values in a min-heap library).
2. While the heap has at least two stones: pop `y`, pop `x`; if `y != x`, push `y − x`.
3. Return the remaining stone, or 0.

Do not re-sort the array each round; that is O(n² log n). With weights up to 1000, a counting array is another option, but the heap is simpler.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(n)
