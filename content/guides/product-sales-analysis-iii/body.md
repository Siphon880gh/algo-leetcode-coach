## Earliest year, all its rows

```sql
SELECT product_id, year AS first_year, quantity, price
FROM Sales
WHERE (product_id, year) IN (
    SELECT product_id, MIN(year)
    FROM Sales
    GROUP BY product_id
);
```

A product may have several sales in its first year, and all of them are returned.

Do not group the outer query and pick one row; that drops the other first-year sales. `RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY product_id ORDER BY year)` and keeping rank 1 also works.

Time: O(n) with hashing  
Space: O(products)
