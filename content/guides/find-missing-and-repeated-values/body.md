## Count

1. cnt[x] for every cell.
2. a = x with cnt 2; b = x in 1 … n² with cnt 0.

Examples → [2, 4], [9, 5].

The sum and the sum of squares also give a − b and a² − b², from which a and b follow in O(1) space.

Time: O(n²)  
Space: O(n²)
