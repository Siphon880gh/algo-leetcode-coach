## Window with counts

1. For each r: cnt[s[r]] += 1; while cnt[s[r]] > 2: cnt[s[l]] −= 1, l += 1.
2. best = max(best, r − l + 1).

Examples → 4, 2.

The limit is per letter, not on distinct letters: allowing at most two distinct letters gives example 1 → 7 instead of 4.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(26)
