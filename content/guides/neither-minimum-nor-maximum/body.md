## Middle of three

1. If `len(nums) < 3`, return −1.
2. Return the middle of `sorted(nums[0..2])`.

Examples → 2, −1, 2.

The middle of three is above one value and below another, so it cannot be the global minimum or maximum. Sorting the whole array also works but is O(n log n) for no gain.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
