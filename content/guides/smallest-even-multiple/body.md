## Parity decides

1. Return `n` if `n` is even, else `2 · n`.

Examples → 10, 6.

In general lcm(a, b) = a · b / gcd(a, b), and gcd(2, n) is 2 for even n and 1 for odd n.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
