## Greedy

1. s > 9n → −1. s = 0 → 0.
2. Each of the n digits from the left takes min(9, remaining sum).

Examples → 90, −1, 0. Matched trying every number for every n ≤ 5 and s ≤ 100.

Filling 9s from the right: example 1 → 9 instead of 90.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
