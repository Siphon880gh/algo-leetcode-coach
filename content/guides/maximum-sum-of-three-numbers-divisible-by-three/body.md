## Buckets

1. b[r] = up to three largest values with remainder r.
2. best = max of each full bucket’s sum and b[0][0] + b[1][0] + b[2][0] when all exist.
3. Return best (0 if none).

Examples → 9, 0. Checked against trying every triple on 3,000 random arrays.

Trying same-remainder triples only: example 1 → 0 instead of 9.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
