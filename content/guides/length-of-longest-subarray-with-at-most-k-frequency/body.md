## Sliding window

1. For each r: count[nums[r]] += 1; while it is > k, drop nums[l] and l += 1.
2. ans = max(ans, r − l + 1).

Examples → 6, 2, 4.

Only nums[r] can exceed k after adding it, so only it needs checking.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
