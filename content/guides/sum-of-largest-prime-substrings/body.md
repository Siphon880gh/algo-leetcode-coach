## Brute force

1. For every substring: v = int(substring) (leading zeros drop out).
2. Keep v in a set if it is prime (trial division to √v).
3. Answer the sum of the three largest in the set (or all of them).

Examples → 1469, 11.

Not deduplicating: example 2 → 22 instead of 11.

Time: O(n² · √V), V < 10^10  
Space: O(n²)
