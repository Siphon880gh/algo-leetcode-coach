## Enumerate

1. For each i, extend j and update y; x = j − i + 1 − y.
2. Count when y > 0 and x · b ≤ y · a.

Examples → 7, 3, 0. Checked against exact fractions on 2,000 random arrays.

Cross-multiplying the wrong way (x · a ≤ y · b): example 1 → 3 instead of 7.

Time: O(n²)  
Space: O(1)
