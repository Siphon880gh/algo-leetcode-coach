## Two masks

1. `even` = popcount(`n & 0x555`); `odd` = popcount(`n & 0x2AA`).
2. Return `[even, odd]`.

Examples → [1,2], [0,1]. Checked against walking the bits for every n up to 1000.

n ≤ 1000 fits in 10 bits, so these masks cover every index; 0x55555555 works for any 32-bit n.

Time: O(1)  
Space: O(1)
