## Prefix extremes

1. For j from m − 1: include nums[j − m + 1] in the running max and min.
2. best = max(best, nums[j] · max, nums[j] · min).

Examples → 81, 20, 35. Checked against every pair of ends on 300 random arrays.

Tracking only the maximum: example 1 → 9 instead of 81.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
