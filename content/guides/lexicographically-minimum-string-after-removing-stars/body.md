## 26 stacks

1. Letter: push its index on its stack.
2. Star: pop from the smallest non-empty stack and mark that index removed.
3. Output the unmarked letters.

Examples → aab, abc. Checked against trying every allowed deletion on 500 random strings.

Removing the leftmost tie fails: example 1 → aba instead of aab.

Time: O(26 n)  
Space: O(n)
