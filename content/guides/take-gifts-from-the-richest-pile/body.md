## Max-heap

1. Heapify `gifts` as a max-heap.
2. Repeat `k` times: pop `x`; push `floor(√x)`.
3. Return the sum of the heap.

Examples → 29, 4.

Use an integer square root to avoid floating-point errors near perfect squares. The total can reach 10^12, so use 64-bit.

Time: O(n + k log n)  
Space: O(n)
