## From the right

1. total[i] = energy[i] + (total[i + k] if i + k < n else 0), for i = n − 1 down to 0.
2. Return max total.

Examples → 3, −1.

You cannot stop early: dropping negative tails with max(0, total[i + k]) gives example 1 → 5 instead of 3.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
