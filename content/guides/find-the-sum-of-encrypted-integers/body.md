## Per number

1. d = largest digit, L = number of digits; encrypt = d · (11…1 with L ones).
2. Return the sum.

Examples → 6, 66.

Keep the length: using just the largest digit gives example 2 → 6 instead of 66.

Time: O(n · digits)  
Space: O(1)
