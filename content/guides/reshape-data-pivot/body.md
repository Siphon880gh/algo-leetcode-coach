## pivot

1. Return `weather.pivot(index='month', columns='city', values='temperature')`.

pivot sorts the labels, giving April, February, January, March, May with ElPaso before Jacksonville, as in the example. Checked in pandas 2.2.

Swapping index and columns gives one row per city (2 × 5 here).

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(n)
