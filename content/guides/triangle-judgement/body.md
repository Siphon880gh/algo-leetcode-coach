## Every pair beats the third

```sql
SELECT x, y, z,
    CASE
        WHEN x + y > z AND x + z > y AND y + z > x THEN 'Yes'
        ELSE 'No'
    END AS triangle
FROM Triangle;
```

The triangle inequality must hold for all three pairs. If the sides were sorted, only `smallest + middle > largest` would need checking, but in SQL testing all three is simpler.

Do not use `>=`; equal sums give a flat segment, not a triangle (`1, 2, 3` is No). Do not test only `x + y > z`; the largest side could be any of the three.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1) beyond the output
