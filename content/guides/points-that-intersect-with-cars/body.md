## Merge

1. Sort by start; keep `ce`, the furthest covered end.
2. For [s, e]: if s > ce add `e − s + 1`; else if e > ce add `e − ce`; update ce.

Examples → 7, 7. Checked against marking points on 300 random inputs.

Summing lengths without merging double counts: example 1 → 13 instead of 7.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(1)
