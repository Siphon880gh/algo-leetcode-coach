## Two searches

1. a, b = the parts of p around *.
2. i = first index of a in s (−1 → false).
3. Answer whether b occurs in s at or after i + len(a).

Examples → true, false, true. Checked against a regular expression on 1,000 random cases.

Looking for b anywhere: s = ba, p = a*b → true instead of false.

Time: O(|s| · |p|)  
Space: O(1)
