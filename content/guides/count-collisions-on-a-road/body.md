## Count cars that end up stopped

1. Strip leading `L`s and trailing `R`s.
2. Return the number of remaining characters that are not `S`.

Examples → 5, 0.

A head-on R L meeting adds 2, and each moving car hitting a stopped one adds 1, which is exactly one per moving car that stops. Everything between the escapers is trapped by a stopped car or a car moving toward it.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(1)
