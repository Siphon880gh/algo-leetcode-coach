## Sort counts

1. counts = letter counts, largest first.
2. Answer the sum of counts after the first k.

Examples → 1, 0, 2. Checked against trying every set of kept letters on 300 random strings.

Deleting the most frequent letters instead: example 3 → 3 instead of 2.

Time: O(n + 26 log 26)  
Space: O(26)
