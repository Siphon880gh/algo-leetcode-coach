## Count

1. cnt = counts of the digits of n.
2. m = the smallest count over digits with cnt > 0.
3. Return the smallest digit d with cnt[d] = m.

Examples → 1, 2. Checked against sorting digits by (count, digit) on 3,000 random n.

Including digits that never appear (count 0): example 1 → 0 instead of 1.

Time: O(log n)  
Space: O(1)
