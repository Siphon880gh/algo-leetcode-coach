## Per word

`' '.join(w.lower() if len(w) ≤ 2 else w[0].upper() + w[1:].lower() for w in title.split(' '))`.

Examples → `Capitalize The Title`, `First Letter of Each Word`, `i Love Leetcode`.

The length rule applies to every word, including the first: Example 3 keeps `i` lowercase.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
