## Two passes

1. pm[i] = max(nums[0 … i]); sm[i] = min(nums[i … n − 1]).
2. Return the first i with pm[i] − sm[i] ≤ k, else −1.

Examples → 3, −1, 0. Checked against direct max and min on 3,000 random arrays.

Using the min of the whole array: example 1 → −1 instead of 3.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
