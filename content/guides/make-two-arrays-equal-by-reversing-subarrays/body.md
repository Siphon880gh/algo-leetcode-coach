## Counts decide

1. Return `Counter(target) == Counter(arr)` (or compare sorted copies).

`[1,2,3,4]` vs `[2,4,1,3]` → true. `[7]` vs `[7]` → true. `[3,7,9]` vs `[3,7,11]` → false.

Do not simulate reversals; adjacent swaps (bubble sort) already prove any permutation is reachable.

Time: O(n)  
Space: O(n)
