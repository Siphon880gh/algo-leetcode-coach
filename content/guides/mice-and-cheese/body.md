## Differences

1. `base = Σ reward2`.
2. Sort `reward1[i] − reward2[i]` in decreasing order.
3. Return `base +` the sum of the first k.

Examples → 15, 2. Checked against every choice of k cheeses on 300 random cases.

The choices are independent, so the k largest gains win even when some are negative; mouse 1 must eat exactly k. Quickselect makes this O(n) on average.

Time: O(n log n)  
Space: O(n)
