## Count

1. For each i, extend j while s[j] = s[i], adding 1 to cnt[(s[i], j − i + 1)].
2. Return the largest length with a count ≥ 3, or −1.

Examples → 2, −1, 1.

Occurrences may overlap: counting whole runs instead gives example 1 → −1 instead of 2.

Time: O(n²)  
Space: O(n²)
